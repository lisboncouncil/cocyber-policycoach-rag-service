## 1. Purpose

This Cybersecurity Policy defines the minimum-security requirements applicable to all information systems, personnel, and processes of [Organisation]. It establishes [Organisation]'s commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information assets in compliance with international cybersecurity standards and applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

[Organisation] is committed to the continual improvement of its Information Security Management System (ISMS) and to adapting security controls to evolving threats and organisational changes.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All information and information systems owned or operated by [Organisation]
- All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff, and third parties who access [Organisation] systems or data
- All devices (managed and unmanaged) used to process [Organisation] information
- All physical locations where [Organisation] information is processed or stored
- All business processes that depend on [Organisation] information systems

Critical and confidential information or systems are defined as those whose compromise of confidentiality, integrity, or availability would cause material harm to [Organisation].

---

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Executive Leadership (CEO / Board)
- Approve this policy and its periodic revisions
- Provide strategic direction and resources for information security
- Demonstrate commitment to the ISMS

### 3.2 Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Own and maintain this policy and all sub-policies
- Report on ISMS performance to executive leadership
- Drive the continual improvement cycle

### 3.3 IT Management
- Implement and operate technical controls defined in sub-policies
- Maintain an up-to-date inventory of all assets and systems
- Report security incidents to the CISO

### 3.4 Department Managers
- Ensure personnel within their area comply with this policy
- Communicate security roles and responsibilities to their teams
- Escalate security concerns and incidents promptly

### 3.5 All Employees and Contractors
- Comply with this policy and all sub-policies
- Complete mandatory security awareness training
- Report suspected security incidents or policy violations immediately

## 4. Policy Principles

### 4.1 Effective Policies and Procedures
[Organisation] maintains documented, reviewed, and communicated security policies and procedures. Ownership and accountability for each policy are clearly defined.

### 4.2 Know the Environment and Manage Risks
[Organisation] maintains an understanding of its critical information systems and their associated risks. A continuous improvement cycle ensures security controls adapt to organisational and environmental changes.

### 4.3 Secure by Design
[Organisation]'s products, services, and infrastructure are built, tested, and maintained with cybersecurity and privacy in mind.

### 4.4 Resilient Infrastructure
Infrastructure supporting critical systems is designed for high availability, with documented recovery objectives (RTO/RPO).

### 4.5 Proactive Posture
[Organisation] patches systems regularly, monitors for newly discovered vulnerabilities, and learns from security events and incidents.

### 4.6 Privacy and Legal Compliance
[Organisation] collects, uses, and stores personal data in accordance with applicable data protection legislation (e.g., GDPR (EU) 2016/679) and provides the necessary technical and organisational measures to ensure compliance.

## 5. Minimum Requirements

All departments of [Organisation] must comply with the following minimum controls:

### 5.1 Governance
- Security roles and responsibilities are defined and communicated to all internal and external stakeholders.
- An up-to-date inventory of all physical devices, software, and systems is maintained.

### 5.2 Asset and Infrastructure Management
- Equipment essential to critical systems is properly maintained with documented maintenance contracts or spare parts provisions.
- Approved anti-malware software is installed and kept up to date on all endpoints identified by [Organisation].
- The corporate network is secured in accordance with the **Network Security Policy (POL-005)**.

### 5.3 Vulnerability and Patch Management
- Operating systems, server software, and applications are patched and updated as described in the **Vulnerability and Patch Management Policy (POL-007)**.

### 5.4 Access Management
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enforced where technically feasible.
- The principle of least privilege is applied to all access grants.
- Password requirements are defined in the **Password Policy (POL-006)**.
- Detailed access controls are defined in the **Access Control Policy (POL-002)**.

### 5.5 Logging
- Critical and confidential systems must log successful and failed authentication events at minimum.

### 5.6 Backup and Recovery
- A documented process exists to recover critical systems following a disaster.
- A documented process exists to restore critical documents or records following accidental deletion.
- Detailed requirements are defined in the **Backup and Recovery Policy (POL-004)**.

### 5.7 Incident Response
- A documented Cyber Incident Response Plan (POL-008) is in place and tested at least annually.

### 5.8 Awareness and Training
- All employees and contractors receive mandatory security awareness training upon onboarding and at least annually thereafter.
- The 10 Golden Rules for Cybersecurity are communicated to all personnel, who must acknowledge and agree to abide by them.

---

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only where a compensating control of equivalent or greater effectiveness is implemented.

### 6.1 Exception Request Process
1. The requesting department submits a written exception request to the CISO, documenting: the control requirement, the business justification, the proposed compensating control, and the requested validity period.
2. The CISO reviews the request and provides a recommendation.
3. Exceptions are approved or rejected by the CISO and, for high-risk exceptions, by executive leadership.
4. Approved exceptions are documented, time-limited (maximum 12 months), and subject to periodic review.
5. All active exceptions are tracked in the ISMS exception register.

---

## 7. Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Violations will be treated according to the severity of the breach:

| Severity | Examples | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Accidental, isolated non-compliance | Documented warning, mandatory retraining |
| Moderate | Repeated non-compliance, negligent behaviour | Formal disciplinary action per HR policy |
| Severe | Deliberate circumvention, data exfiltration | Dismissal, potential legal action |
| Criminal | Intentional damage, fraud | Reporting to law enforcement |

Violations are documented in the ISMS incident register and reviewed during management review.

## 9. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **ISMS** | Information Security Management System — the set of policies, procedures, and controls used to manage information security. |
| **Confidentiality** | Ensuring that information is accessible only to those authorised to access it. |
| **Integrity** | Safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods. |
| **Availability** | Ensuring that authorised users have access to information and associated assets when required. |
| **Critical system** | A system whose disruption would cause material operational or reputational harm to [Organisation]. |
| **RTO** | Recovery Time Objective — the maximum acceptable time to restore a system after a disruption. |
| **RPO** | Recovery Point Objective — the maximum acceptable period of data loss following a disruption. |
